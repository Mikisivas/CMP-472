## Working rules

- Check-in meeting twice a week (15 minutes). Sub-team leads attend, everyone welcome. Secretary posts minutes in `docs/minutes/`.
- Every task lives on the task board with one owner and a due date.
- Developers work on branches and open pull requests. The tech lead reviews before merging.
- Anyone blocked for more than one day tells their sub-team lead.
